A few things to keep in mind
  • Place diyas and havan cups on a stable, heat-resistant surface, away from curtains, paper and other flammable materials.
  • Never leave an open flame unattended. Keep away from children and pets.
  • Allow hot lamps and metal items to cool before handling.
  • Use dhoop and havan cups in a well-ventilated area.
  • Store the kit in a cool, dry place away from moisture.
  • Handle brass items with care; they may become hot during use.
